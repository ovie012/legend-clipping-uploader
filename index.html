Legend Clipping Uploader

by Legend Da Creator (legenddacreator)

Legend Clipping Uploader is a small personal tool. It runs on the owner's own computer and uploads short videos made by Legend Da Creator to Legend Da Creator's own accounts on YouTube and TikTok.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-ups, no other users, and it never posts to anyone else's account.

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos to the legenddacreator YouTube channel, either public or scheduled for a set time.
  • Sends finished videos to the legenddacreator TikTok account as drafts, so the owner reviews and posts each one by hand in the TikTok app.
  • Adds the title, description, hashtags and the paid-partnership disclosure that each video needs.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, messages or followers.
  • It does not collect data from viewers or from other people's accounts.
  • It does not sell, share or send any data to anyone.

Permissions it asks for

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (the youtube.upload scope) only.
  • TikTok: permission to send videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (the video.upload scope) and the basic account information TikTok gives with sign-in.
