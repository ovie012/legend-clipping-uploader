Privacy Policy

Legend Clipping Uploader, by Legend Da Creator

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who this covers

Legend Clipping Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner, Legend Da Creator, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. No one else signs in to it.

Information the app uses

  • Sign-in tokens from Google (YouTube) and TikTok, which let the app upload on the owner's behalf.
  • Basic account information that TikTok returns at sign-in (such as the account's open ID and display name), used only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The videos and their text (titles, descriptions, hashtags) that the owner chooses to upload.

Where it is kept

All of this stays on the owner's own computer, in files only the owner's user account can open. The app has no server and no database. Videos and their text are sent only to YouTube or TikTok, at the owner's request, to be uploaded.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information with anyone. It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and it sets no cookies. This website sets no cookies either.

Google and YouTube data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Sign-in tokens are kept only while the app is in use. Access can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services. Deleting the token files on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.

Children

The app is not meant for children and is used only by its adult owner.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the contact page.
